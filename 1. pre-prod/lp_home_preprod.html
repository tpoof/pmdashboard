      How LEAF works

      Build forms, workflows, and reports in a few easy steps.

      No coding required. LEAF gives every VA team the tools to digitize and automate any business process.

      Step 01 — Design

      Design your form

      Our user-friendly, drag-and-drop Form Editor creates a custom form for your process — no developers needed.

      • Dozens of question formats to choose from
      • Toggle questions on or off based on previous responses
      • Integration with VA Active Directory
      Step 02 — Automate

      Build your workflow

      Integrated, interactive flowcharts automatically route every request to the right approver at every step.

      • Notify requestors and approvers every time something happens
      • Clear ownership of each step in the process
      • Sub-forms and workspaces for multi-part processes
      Step 03 — Analyze

      Run analytics

      Use powerful built-in tools to gain insights and make faster, better-informed decisions.

      • Report Builder — drill into key data, with options to export to Excel
      • Timeline Explorer — find delays and gaps in your process at a glance
      • Data Visualizer — surface patterns in what your submitters are entering
