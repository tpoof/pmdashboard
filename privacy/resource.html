The LEAF platform provides tools, references, and platform-level enhancements that enable local LEAF Process Owners to support the VA Privacy Program. The resources below are intended to help teams understand available guidance and incorporate privacy considerations directly into their workflows.

    LEAF Secure (LEAF-S)

    What is LEAF-S

    LEAF-S is a secure variant of the LEAF platform designed for workflows that contain protected health information (PHI) or personally identifiable information (PII). It exists to protect sensitive data with tighter controls and visibility.

    Once certified, your site receives LEAF-S controls and will be monitored for continued compliance with sensitive data handling standards.

    What to expect on a LEAF-S site

    LEAF-S sites automatically log out after 15 minutes of inactivity.

    Pro tip: Keep forms in short sections. LEAF autosaves after each section, so progress is less likely to be lost if a session times out.

    When you need LEAF-S

    LEAF-S is required only for sites that collect, display, or route PHI or PII.

    Common examples include:

    • Full name combined with date of birth
    • Social Security Number (full or partial, depending on the process)
    • Medical or health information
    • Case details tied to an identifiable Veteran
    • Attachments containing identifiable information
    • Employee records

    How to get started

    1. Path 1: Once you identify a sensitive field in your form, a banner appears in the Form Editor with a link to the LEAF-S certification form.
    2. Path 2: Admin Panel > Site Settings > Start Certification.

    The LEAF-S certification process will automatically route the request to your local Privacy Officer. They will need to review and approve before a site is LEAF-S certified.

    Need more detail or support? Visit the LEAF-S Help Library for step-by-step guidance.
