Our listening channels

How We Listen

We collect feedback through five complementary channels so no voice goes unheard — whether you have 30 seconds or 30 minutes.

Surveys & Satisfaction Scores

Periodic CSAT and pulse surveys measure how satisfied users are with the platform, support, and training — giving us quantifiable benchmarks to track over time.

Open-Ended Written Feedback

Free-text comment fields attached to every survey and support interaction surface the nuance that numbers alone can't capture — the specific pain points and praise that drive real change.

Support Ticket Themes

Our support team tags and categorizes every ticket so we can identify recurring problems, prioritize fixes, and proactively address systemic issues before they reach scale.

User Interviews & Focus Groups

One-on-one interviews and small-group sessions give us deep, contextual understanding of how specific teams use LEAF — and where the experience breaks down for them.

Feature Requests & Ideas

Through the LEAF Idea Portal, any VA employee can submit, vote on, and track feature requests — giving the community a direct line into the product roadmap.

Our process

How It Works

From the moment you share feedback to the moment a change ships, every step is intentional.

Step 01

Collect

Feedback flows in continuously through surveys, support tickets, interviews, and the Idea Portal — all routed into a unified system.

Step 02

Analyze

Our team reviews themes, tracks trends over time, and scores feedback by frequency and impact to identify what matters most.

Step 03

Act & Report

Prioritized insights go directly into the product roadmap and support improvements — and we report back to the community on what changed and why.

Closing the loop

What Happens With Your Feedback

Submitting feedback shouldn't feel like sending a message into a void. Here's exactly what happens after you share input with us.

Received & Logged

Every piece of feedback — regardless of channel — is captured and tagged in our tracking system within one business day.

Categorized & Themed

Our team groups feedback by topic, urgency, and affected workflow — surfacing patterns individual responses alone would never reveal.

Prioritized by Impact

Themes are scored by frequency, severity, and strategic alignment. High-impact items go directly to the product and support teams.

Acted On

Feedback-driven changes are tracked through development and release. Every shipped improvement tied to your input is recorded.

Communicated Back

We publish regular VoC reports and release notes that explicitly connect changes to the feedback that drove them — so you know your voice made a difference.
