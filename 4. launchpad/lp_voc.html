Our listening channels

How We Listen

We collect feedback through six complementary channels so no voice goes unheard, whether you have 30 seconds or 30 minutes.

Surveys & Satisfaction Scores

Periodic Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) and pulse surveys measure satisfaction and track trends over time.

Open-Ended Written Feedback

Free-text comments surface the nuance and specific pain points numbers alone can't capture.

Support Ticket Themes

Every ticket is tagged to identify recurring problems and systemic issues.

User Interviews & Focus Groups

One-on-one conversations give us deep, contextual understanding of actual workflows.

Feature Requests & Ideas

Submit, vote on, and track ideas through the LEAF Idea Portal.

Community of Practice (CoP)

Direct feedback taken live during Open Office Hours and CoP calls.

Closing the loop

What Happens With Your Feedback

Submitting feedback shouldn't feel like sending a message into a void. Here's exactly what happens after you share input with us.

Received & Logged

Every piece of feedback is captured and tagged within one business day.

Categorized & Themed

Feedback is grouped by topic, urgency, and workflow to surface patterns.

Prioritized by Impact

Themes are scored by frequency, severity, and strategic alignment.

Acted On

Changes are tracked through development and release.

Communicated Back

We publish VoC reports that connect changes to the feedback that drove them.
