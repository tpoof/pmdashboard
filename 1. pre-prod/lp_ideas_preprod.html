    How the Idea Portal Works

    Every idea moves through the same simple lifecycle, from the moment you submit it to a possible outcome.

    1

    Submission

    You share your idea with a title, description, benefit, category, and impact. You can save it as a draft first and come back to finish it anytime before submitting.

    2

    Review

    Our team reviews every idea that's submitted. Community votes help show which ideas resonate most, and idea status updates as review progresses.

    3

    Possible Implementation

    Ideas that move forward are prioritized based on available resources and organizational priorities. Not every idea can be implemented, but every idea is genuinely considered.
