    Sections

    Click rate is the share of visits that saw a section and then clicked something in it. Sections count as seen after about a second in the middle of the screen. Sections with nothing to click always show 0%.

    Most clicked

    Top 15 links and buttons by total clicks.

    Where people go next

    After viewing the starting page, where the visit went next. "Left or stayed" covers closing the tab, following a link to another site, or simply not going further.

    Key actions

    Clicks on the main calls to action, wherever they appear.

    Finder

    Typed searches are only counted as a match or no match. The text people type is never recorded.

    Where visits come from

    A bounce is a visit with one page view and no clicks. Links shared with &src=name on the end show up as tagged links.

    Screen sizes

    Visits by screen width.

    Pages viewed

    Views of each page. Broken links are views of a page that does not exist.

    Page load time

    Time until a page's content appears. Home loads with the Launchpad itself and is not measured.

    Friction

    Dead click: clicking something that looks clickable but does nothing. Rage click: four or more fast clicks on the same control. Load failure: a page that did not load. Script error: the file and line where the page's code failed.

    Internal use only. Counts are directional: visits that could not be recorded are missing.
